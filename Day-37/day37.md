# Day 37 – Claude AI Challenge: Task Compass

## 60 Days of Claude AI Challenge

## Overview

For **Day 37** of the **60 Days Claude AI Challenge**, I built **Task Compass**, an interactive application that simulates how work flows through real organizations.

The project focuses on three core concepts:
- **Ownership** – Assigning the right primary owner for different workplace scenarios.
- **Task Routing** – Understanding the correct sequence of teams involved in completing a task.
- **Collaboration** – Identifying when multiple departments should work together to solve complex organizational problems.

This exercise helped strengthen my understanding of organizational workflows, accountability, delegation, and cross-functional collaboration.

# Organizational Thinking Dashboard

### Understanding Scores

- **Ownership:** 56%
- **Delegation:** 58%
- **Collaboration:** 56%
- **Workflow Thinking:** 17%

### Reflection

**Strengths**
- Demonstrated a solid understanding of handling unfamiliar organizational situations.
- Identified ownership correctly across most scenarios.

**Areas for Improvement**
- Improve workflow thinking by focusing more on the sequence in which teams collaborate.
- Avoid assigning support roles as primary owners.
- Better recognize when multiple departments should be involved in larger organizational decisions.


# Key Learnings

- Clear ownership creates accountability and reduces confusion.
- Delegation supports execution without transferring accountability.
- Effective collaboration involves the right stakeholders rather than every stakeholder.
- Organizational workflows are most efficient when tasks follow the correct routing between departments.
- Understanding responsibilities across teams leads to better decision-making and smoother execution.
