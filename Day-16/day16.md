# 60 Days of Claude AI Challenge
# Day 16 -- Build Your First Stock Research Skill

## 📌 Overview

This task focused on using a custom Claude AI workflow to generate a
structured stock research report and visualize insights in a simple,
understandable way.



## ⚙️ Custom AI Skill Used

-   Data extraction and summarization using Claude\
-   Structuring raw financial information into readable insights\
-   Generating simplified explanations for complex data\
-   Converting analysis into visual-ready content (poster + charts)



## 📊 Generated Stock Report (TCS)

### Key Highlights

-   Strong business performance with consistent profits\
-   Zero debt and stable financial position\
-   High return ratios indicating efficient operations

### Observations

-   Despite strong fundamentals, stock valuation appears lower\
-   Growth slowdown impacts market perception\
-   Market behavior doesn't always align with company strength



## 📈 Comparison Insights

-   **Strong company vs stock performance gap**
-   Businesses can perform well, but stock movement depends on multiple
    factors\
-   Growth expectations play a major role in valuation



## 🧠 Key Learnings

-   AI can simplify complex datasets into easy insights\
-   Visualization makes analysis more understandable\
-   "Good company" does not always mean "good stock performance"\
-   AI helps in connecting patterns, not just presenting data



## 🚀 Final Reflection

This task showed how AI can assist not just in generating answers, but
in: - Structuring thought processes\
- Identifying patterns\
- Presenting insights clearly

Using Claude made the entire workflow faster, more organized, and easier
to interpret.
