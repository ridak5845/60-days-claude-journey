# Solved Case Report

## Case

**Verdant Skincare**

### Objective

Introduce a premium skincare serum to a younger demographic.


## Investigation Findings

### Campaign Performance

- Reach: **2.1M**
- CTR: **2.4%**
- Engagement: **14.5%**
- Conversion Rate: **0.5%**
- Sales: **$9,300**


### Customer Feedback

- Product considered too expensive.
- Strong influencer appeal.
- Demand for smaller trial-size products.
- Price identified as the biggest purchase barrier.


### Root Cause

The campaign successfully generated awareness and engagement but targeted a budget-conscious audience with a premium-priced product and no affordable entry option.

The issue was **not** the marketing creative—it was the mismatch between audience expectations and pricing strategy.


## Recommended Improvements

1. Introduce lower-cost trial-size products.
2. Align pricing with audience purchasing power.
3. Test higher-income customer segments.
4. Improve value communication throughout the campaign.
5. Support premium pricing with stronger product education.


# Key Learnings

- Marketing success depends on aligning audience, pricing, and product positioning.
- High engagement does not guarantee strong conversions.
- Customer feedback often reveals the true cause of performance issues.
- Data should guide marketing decisions instead of assumptions.
- Budget allocation must support channels that generate both engagement and conversions.
- Root cause analysis is essential before making optimization decisions.
- Interactive investigations help improve analytical thinking and marketing strategy skills.
