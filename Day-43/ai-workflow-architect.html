Wrap-up

Workflow summary

This workflow takes an agency from a signed client to a repeatable, reportable monthly cadence across 10 stages: discovery, strategy, calendar planning, production, approvals, publishing, community management, analytics, reporting, and optimization/upsell. The throughline is that AI compresses the first draft — strategy briefs, captions, visuals, replies, reports — while a human account lead owns judgment, brand risk, and the client relationship at every checkpoint.

Run per-client at a monthly cadence for stages 1–3 and 9–10, and a weekly/daily cadence for stages 4–8. A lean two-person pod (strategist + creator/community manager) can realistically run 6–10 client accounts using this stack before needing to add headcount.
