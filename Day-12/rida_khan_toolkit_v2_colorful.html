Q: Power BI/Tableau listed as required — are you proficient?
Prep: Be honest — "I'm actively learning through [platform]. I work with data visualization concepts and Excel charts today. I'm confident I'll be productive in Power BI within my first month."

Prep: Use the IBM internship — describe the dataset, issues found (nulls, duplicates, outliers), what you did in pandas, and how model performance improved after cleaning.
Q: How do you define a KPI for a business team?
Prep: "Start with the business question — what decision needs to be made? Then work backwards to what's measurable, what data exists, and what threshold means success." Use social media app engagement rate as an example.
