## 60 Days of Claude AI Challenge

## Project Overview

For Day 26 of the **60 Days of Claude AI Challenge**, I built a **Prior Authorization Workflow Simulator** to understand how the US healthcare prior authorization process works from referral to final decision.

The simulator provides an interactive learning experience that walks through the complete workflow involving the **Patient**, **Provider**, and **Payer**, while demonstrating the decisions and documentation required before treatment approval.

# Key Learnings

- Prior Authorization is a structured process involving coordination between the patient, provider, and payer.
- Medical necessity must be supported with appropriate clinical evidence before authorization can be granted.
- Complete and accurate documentation helps reduce delays during the review process.
- Depending on the review outcome, a request may be approved, pended, denied, appealed, or require a peer-to-peer review.
- Visualizing the workflow made it much easier to understand how each stage contributes to the final decision and impacts the patient's waiting time.
