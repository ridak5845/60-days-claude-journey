# 60 Days Claude AI Challenge

# Day 22 – Validate Your Startup Idea Like a VC

## Challenge Overview

As part of the **60 Days of Claude AI Challenge**, today's task was to **Validate Your Startup Idea Like a VC: Turn Startup Assumptions into Evidence-Based Insights**.

The goal was to analyze a startup idea from an investor's perspective by evaluating market opportunity, customer demand, competitive landscape, risks, and scalability before investing time and resources into building the product.


## Startup Idea

### Food Waste Marketplace

Food Waste Marketplace is a platform that helps restaurants, cafes, bakeries, hotels, and grocery stores sell or donate surplus food instead of discarding it.

Consumers can purchase quality food at discounted prices, while NGOs and food banks can receive food donations, creating social, environmental, and economic value.


## Problem Statement

Millions of kilograms of edible food are wasted every day while many individuals struggle to access affordable meals and NGOs face food shortages.

The platform aims to bridge this gap by connecting food providers with consumers and organizations that can utilize surplus food before it goes to waste.

## Startup Insights

### Key Insight #1

A large problem does not automatically guarantee customer adoption.

### Key Insight #2

Merchant onboarding and operational simplicity are critical for marketplace success.

### Key Insight #3

Food safety and regulatory compliance are major factors that can influence growth.

### Key Insight #4

Combining discounted food sales with NGO donations creates a unique market position.


## Assumption Tested

### Assumption

Restaurants would readily join a food waste platform because it helps reduce waste.

### Result

Challenged ❌

### Why?

The analysis revealed that restaurant owners may have concerns regarding:

* Food safety
* Brand reputation
* Additional operational effort
* Pickup logistics
* Staff training requirements

This highlighted the importance of validating merchant demand before scaling.
