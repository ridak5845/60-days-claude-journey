# Day 28 – Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator


### Project Overview

The Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator is an interactive application that demonstrates the operational workflow required before a patient can be admitted to a hospital. It simulates the coordination between administrative staff, physicians, nursing teams, and insurance providers while tracking overall admission readiness.

## Key Learnings

- Hospital admission requires coordination across multiple departments.
- Prior Authorization is only one part of the overall admission process.
- Insurance verification, documentation, consent, and physician approvals all contribute to admission readiness.
- Bed availability directly impacts operational efficiency.
- Readiness dashboards help identify bottlenecks before patient admission.
- Completing tasks in the correct sequence reduces delays and improves workflow visibility.
