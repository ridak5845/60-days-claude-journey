Money tips for student allowances

The 50/30/20 split

Roughly 50% needs (food, transport), 30% wants (fun, shopping), 20% savings. Adjust to fit a fixed allowance.

Pay yourself first

Move your savings amount aside the day allowance lands — before it has a chance to get spent.

Track small spends

₹50 a day on snacks is ₹1,500 a month. Small daily spends are usually the biggest leak.

Separate needs from wants

Before buying, ask: would I still buy this if it wasn't on sale or in front of me right now?

Build a tiny buffer

Even ₹500 set aside covers a surprise cost without derailing the whole month's budget.

Review, don't restrict

A weekly 5-minute review beats a rigid budget you'll abandon by week two.
