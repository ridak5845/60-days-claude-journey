# 60 Days Claude AI Challenge

# Day 25 – AI Shark Tank Simulator 

## Challenge Overview
Today's challenge was to build an **AI Shark Tank Simulator** and pitch a startup idea to AI investors. The goal was to defend the business model, answer tough investor questions, and receive an investment decision based on market potential, scalability, and profitability.


## Startup Pitched: FoodSaver AI 

FoodSaver AI is an AI-powered platform that helps:

- Food businesses monetize surplus food instead of wasting it
- Budget-conscious consumers access affordable meals
- NGOs and food banks find available food donations in real time
- Reduce food waste while creating economic and social impact

### Revenue Model
- Commission on orders
- Subscription plans for businesses
- Premium analytics dashboard

## Toughest Investor Questions

1. Why would customers choose FoodSaver AI over existing alternatives?
2. What stops a well-funded competitor from copying the model?
3. Where does the actual profit come from after support and infrastructure costs?
4. How does the business scale beyond its initial customer segments?
5. What happens if the platform goes down?


## Key Learnings

### Business Lessons
- A good idea is not enough; execution matters more.
- Investors focus heavily on scalability and defensibility.
- Unit economics must make sense from day one.
- Distribution can be a stronger moat than technology.

### Founder Lessons
- Every assumption should be defendable with logic.
- Customer acquisition and retention are critical.
- Solving a real problem creates long-term value.
- Strong partnerships can accelerate growth.


## Reflection

This challenge helped me think like both a founder and an investor. Instead of focusing only on the product, I had to justify the market size, business model, profitability, competition strategy, and long-term vision. It was a great exercise in understanding what makes a startup truly investable.
