## Project Overview

This interactive Supply Chain Optimizer allows users to design and evaluate a complete supply chain by making strategic decisions across multiple operational stages. Every choice influences key business metrics such as cost efficiency, delivery speed, operational risk, customer satisfaction, and sustainability.

The simulation concludes with an optimization dashboard that provides an overall supply chain score, highlights strengths and weaknesses, identifies major risks, and recommends practical improvements.


## Optimization Dashboard Highlights

- Overall Supply Chain Performance Score
- Cost Efficiency Analysis
- Delivery Speed Metrics
- Operational Risk Assessment
- Customer Satisfaction Rating
- Sustainability Score
- Strengths & Weaknesses Summary
- Personalized Optimization Recommendations


## Key Learnings

- Supply chain optimization requires balancing cost, speed, resilience, and sustainability.
- Diversifying suppliers can reduce operational risk while increasing flexibility.
- Factory location significantly impacts production costs and delivery times.
- Warehouse placement directly affects fulfillment speed and customer experience.
- Transportation methods should be selected based on both cost and delivery requirements.
- Maintaining the right inventory levels helps avoid both stockouts and unnecessary storage costs.
- Data-driven decision-making leads to more efficient and resilient supply chain operations.
