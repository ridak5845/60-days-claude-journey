# Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab


**Project:** Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab

An interactive single-file React simulation where players become the Chief Supply Chain Officer of a fictional enterprise and navigate a major supply chain disruption through strategic decision-making, supplier negotiations, executive leadership, AI investments, and performance analysis.


# Project Overview

Operation Lifeline is designed to make supply chain concepts accessible to complete beginners while still presenting realistic business trade-offs.

Players progress through seven stages:

1. Company Profile
2. Crisis Detection
3. War Room Strategy
4. Supplier Negotiation
5. CEO Boardroom
6. AI Strategy
7. Crisis Debrief Dashboard

Every playthrough generates a different company, crisis scenario, business metrics, and final outcome.


# Features

- Random fictional company generation
- Multiple crisis scenarios
- Interactive war room decision making
- Animated business KPI dashboard
- Supplier negotiation simulator
- CEO leadership assessment
- AI investment planning
- Executive performance dashboard
- Personalized recommendations
- Fully responsive enterprise UI
- Offline single HTML application


# Technology Used

### Key Learnings

#### Supply chains depend on preparation.

Organizations with diversified suppliers recover significantly faster than those relying on a single source.


#### Every decision has trade-offs.

Reducing cost often impacts customer satisfaction, while improving speed may reduce profitability.


#### Communication matters.

Keeping customers informed during disruptions protects trust and brand reputation.


#### AI improves resilience.

Predictive analytics, supplier monitoring, and procurement automation reduce future disruption risks.


#### Leadership extends beyond operations.

Effective crisis management requires balancing financial performance, operational continuity, employee coordination, and customer expectations.
