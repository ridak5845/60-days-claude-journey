60 Days Claude AI Challenge — Day 6

As part of my **60 Days Claude AI Challenge**, Day 6 was focused on improving my resume using **ATS (Applicant Tracking System) Resume Optimization** techniques. The goal was to understand how ATS scanners evaluate resumes and make strategic improvements that increase visibility for Data Science and AI roles.

Using ATS Resume Optimization in Claude AI my ATS score improved from 42 to 83, reflecting a significant enhancement in how my resume is parsed and evaluated.

—>My original resume had an ATS score of:

**42/100**

Although it contained my education, project, and certification details, it lacked several ATS-friendly elements:

- Missing professional summary
- Limited Data Science & AI keywords
- Weak experience presentation
- Inconsistent formatting
- Missing academic details such as CGPA and complete educational history
- Skills section not aligned with ATS keyword matching

—>Optimization Process

Using ATS optimization principles, I revised my resume by focusing on:

Key Changes That Increased My Score-

- Added Professional Summary (+12)
The biggest improvement came from including a targeted summary packed with relevant Data Science and AI keywords.

- Improved Education Section (+9)
Adding CGPA, graduation timeline, and academic percentages provided more complete information for ATS systems.

- Structured Internship Experience (+8)
Presenting the IBM Machine Learning Internship as experience increased the weight of practical exposure.

- Better Keyword Matching (+8)
Including Machine Learning and Data Science-related technologies helped align the resume with job descriptions.

- ATS-Friendly Formatting (+8)
Cleaner formatting made the resume easier for ATS software to parse.

—>ATS Score Improvement:-

| Metric | Before | After |
| ATS Score | 42/100 | 83/100 |
| Improvement | - | +41 Points |

**Final Result:** My resume score increased from **42 to 83**, representing a **97.6% improvement** in ATS performance.

—> What I Learned:-
- ATS systems prioritize keywords and structure over design.
- A strong professional summary significantly improves visibility.
- Properly presenting internships and projects boosts ATS rankings.
- Complete education details and relevant skills increase matching scores.
- Clean, ATS-friendly formatting improves resume parsing.

 —>Key Takeaways:-

- ATS optimization can dramatically improve resume visibility.
- Keyword-rich summaries increase recruiter search relevance.
- Properly showcasing internships and projects boosts experience scores.
- Strong action verbs make achievements more impactful.
- Education details should be complete and clearly presented.
- ATS-friendly formatting is just as important as content.
- Small changes can produce major improvements in ATS performance.
