# Key Learnings

- Real-time supply chain management requires balancing multiple KPIs rather than optimizing a single metric.
- Every operational disruption can have cascading effects across inventory, transportation, customer satisfaction, and revenue.
- Prioritizing high-impact alerts first helps minimize business risk.
- Data-driven decision-making enables faster and more effective responses during disruptions.
- Interactive simulations are an excellent way to understand operational trade-offs and build strategic thinking skills.
- A centralized Control Tower improves visibility across the supply chain and supports proactive decision-making.
