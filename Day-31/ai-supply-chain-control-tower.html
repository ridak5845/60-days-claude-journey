How to Play

Head of Operations — Briefing

Alerts appear as operational issues hit your supply chain. Each has a priority (High / Medium / Low) and a countdown — respond before it expires or it resolves itself, badly.

  • Pick an action for each alert. Different actions cost different amounts and affect KPIs differently.
  • The "best" action for a given alert improves KPIs and raises your score. Weaker choices help less, or hurt.
  • Ignoring or delaying can be cheap short-term but often costs you later, sometimes with a delayed penalty.
  • As the 3-minute shift goes on, alerts arrive faster and more can be active at once.
  • Keep an eye on Service Level, Satisfaction, Inventory Health, Transport Efficiency, Operating Cost, and Revenue Protected — they all roll into your final grade.
