### **60 Days of Claude AI Challenge**

### **Day 11 - ATS Resume Optimization with Claude AI**

**Objective**

Analyze my resume against a target Data Analyst Job Description using Claude AI, identify ATS gaps, and generate an optimized ATS-friendly resume.

 
#### **ATS Analysis Summary**

Initial ATS Match Score
72/100

#### **Strengths Identified**

•Strong alignment with SQL and Python requirements.
•Experience with data cleaning and validation.
•Exposure to structured data management and analytics concepts.
•Relevant academic background in Computer Science.

#### **Key Gaps Found**

•Power BI / Tableau not sufficiently highlighted.
•Excel skills not emphasized.
•Statistics concepts not clearly mentioned.
•Dashboard creation experience missing.
•KPI and stakeholder-related terminology absent.
•SQL skills listed but not demonstrated through projects or experience.

#### **Resume Improvements Made**

-Added Data Analysis-focused keywords.
-Highlighted SQL querying, reporting, joins, and aggregations.
-Included KPI Definition and Statistics in the skills section.
-Added Power BI/Tableau (Learning) and Excel.
-Strengthened data cleaning and validation experience.
-Improved alignment between resume content and job description requirements.

#### **Key Learning**

One of the biggest ATS insights I discovered is that listing a skill alone is not enough. ATS systems and recruiters look for evidence of how a skill was applied in projects or work experience.
For example:
-Instead of only mentioning SQL, it is more effective to describe SQL querying, reporting, joins, aggregations, and data management tasks performed during projects or internships.
-Including relevant industry keywords such as Data Analysis, Dashboards, KPI, Statistics, Excel, and Power BI can significantly improve ATS matching.

#### **Outcome**

This exercise helped me understand how ATS systems evaluate resumes and how strategic keyword placement can improve visibility for Data Analyst opportunities.
