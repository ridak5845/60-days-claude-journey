## Key Customer Insights

- Food providers need a **quick and simple way** to list surplus food.
- Consumers prioritize **affordable pricing, trust, and convenience**.
- NGOs need **predictable food availability and easier coordination**.
- Reducing operational effort is critical for provider adoption.


## MVP Recommendations

### Build First
- WhatsApp/web-based food listing
- Photo upload for surplus food
- Quantity and pickup window selection
- Nearby deal discovery
- Basic trust and verification system

### Avoid Initially
- Native mobile apps
- Delivery fleet
- AI recommendations
- Advanced logistics systems


## Key Learnings

1. Validate customer demand before building features.
2. Simple MVPs can generate stronger insights than complex products.
3. Customer interviews are essential before scaling.
4. Focus on one pilot area before expanding.
5. Solving a clear customer pain point creates the highest adoption potential.


## Final Verdict

🟡 **Promising but Unvalidated**

The problem is significant and the customer segments are well-defined, but real-world validation through interviews and pilot testing is required before investing heavily in development.
