The Value Comment formula: Agree with a specific point → Add one insight or example from your own experience → Ask a genuine question back.

Day 5
Publish Post #2 — lessons-learned / hot take
▶
Unpopular opinion after building 20+ small projects: the hardest part was never the code. It was deciding what "done" actually means before I started — what counts as tested, what counts as documented, what counts as shippable. Every project on my build challenge now goes through the same 3 checks before I call it finished: syntax validation, structure validation, and an actual end-to-end test. Boring? Yes. Has it saved me from shipping broken things? Also yes. What's your "done" checklist look like?
Day 6
Engage with comments + send 5 more connections
▶

Reply to every comment on both posts within a few hours — early engagement is what LinkedIn's algorithm uses to decide whether to keep showing your post. Repeat Day 3's targeting for 5 more connections, this time prioritizing anyone who engaged with your posts.

Day 7
Review profile views & connection acceptance rate
▶

What the numbers mean: A jump in profile views after Day 1 confirms the headline/keyword change is working — that's search visibility. A low connection-acceptance rate (under ~40%) usually means the message felt generic or was sent to the wrong audience — tighten targeting before sending more. Post engagement (comments > likes) is the strongest signal your content is landing, since comments are what the algorithm rewards most.
