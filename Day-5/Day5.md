60 Days Claude AI Challenge — Day 5


Today, I learned about Context Engineering.Context Engineering is the practice of selecting, organizing, and delivering the right information to an AI model at the right time so it can generate accurate, relevant, and useful responses.

📌 Observation:
While learning Context Engineering, I noticed that the quality of an AI's output heavily depends on how information is structured, prioritized, and provided as context. Similarly, Git helps organize and track the evolution of that knowledge in a systematic way.

📚 Learning:
• Context Engineering is about giving AI the right information at the right time.
• Well-structured context leads to more accurate and relevant AI responses.
• Managing context effectively is becoming as important as prompt writing.
• GitHub provides a reliable way to version, document, and track my learning journey.
• Consistently recording progress helps build a reusable knowledge base for future AI projects.

🚀 Key Takeaway: Learning Context Engineering taught me that AI performance is driven not just by prompts, but by the quality of context. Uploading my work to GitHub reinforced the importance of maintaining a structured and trackable learning process.


After learning about context engineering, I conducted a simple experiment with Claude AI by asking it to create a 30-day learning roadmap using two different prompts.

Prompt 1: I simply asked Claude to generate a 30-day roadmap without providing any personal background or learning goals.
Result: The roadmap was generic and designed for a broad audience. While it was useful, it lacked personalization and didn't account for my current skill level or specific objectives.

Prompt 2: I provided detailed context, including: • My learning goal (Context Engineering) • My current stage as a learner • What I already knew • What I wanted to achieve by the end of 30 days
Result: The roadmap became significantly more structured, relevant, and actionable. The tasks were tailored to my experience level, the learning sequence was more logical, and the recommendations aligned closely with my objectives.
